# Harmonic Beacon + Personal Myth Projection (PMP): 5-Minute Presentation Spine

> Slide-by-slide spine for a 5-minute presentation to advisors, intermediaries, and potential strategic backers. Not a final business plan, not financial or legal advice, and not a clinical claim.

## Slide 1 — Deep Inner Journeys Entered Premium Demand

**On slide**

Deep inner journeys moved from niche to premium demand. Hospitality still lacks a careful, substance-free format it can integrate.

**Speaker note**

In just a few years, deep inner journeys moved from cultural niche to international premium demand: mental health went mainstream, the psychedelic renaissance legitimized inner work culturally, and wellness tourism learned to sell it. The supply side did not keep up. The deepest formats remain tied to substances or clinical settings, while what hospitality can actually integrate often stays generic.

## Slide 2 — The Structural Gap Is Commercially Legible Depth

**On slide**

The gap: guided, substance-free experiences, commercially legible and careful enough to become a category of their own.

**Speaker note**

Premium operators are left without a format to offer precisely when their guests began asking for one. Harmonic Beacon + Personal Myth Projection (PMP) is built for that gap: a premium, substance-free, sound-symbolic wellness experience supported by a proprietary harmonic sound field and a structured symbolic process.

## Slide 3 — Beacon + PMP Is A Signature Wellness Experience

**On slide**

Beacon creates a proprietary harmonic field. PMP gives the experience symbolic structure. The format is wellness, not therapy.

**Speaker note**

Beacon is a device and protocol for creating a proprietary harmonic field through sound under situated listening. PMP brings a structured symbolic process with over 15 years of commercial and professional practice behind it. In Costa Rica, the combined format is framed deliberately as non-clinical wellness: exploration, care, and integration, without medical or therapeutic claims.

## Slide 4 — The Discovery Came From Use

**On slide**

Beacon began as a HIT experimental probe. Edge City showed qualitative pull: people returned, queued, and reported positive subjective effects.

**Speaker note**

Beacon began as an experimental probe for Harmonic Information Theory: a way to make harmonic structure perceptible in embodied listening. Its first purpose was research, not wellness commerce. During exploratory sessions, especially at Edge City 2025, an unexpected pattern emerged: people returned, queued to experience the device again, and reported positive subjective effects. Those reports are experiential accounts, not clinical evidence. They explain why we began testing Beacon as a disciplined wellness format.

## Slide 5 — The Team And Operational Base Are Real; Paid Pilots Are Next

**On slide**

Exists today: seven-member core Beacon team, Julián De La Reta's PMP lineage of over 15 years, sold PMP training, Beacon artifact/protocol, free exploratory implementations, market research, team in Costa Rica.

Current stage: pre-first-paid-pilot, placing the first paid pilots now.

**Speaker note**

The base is real. The core Beacon team combines HIT authorship, harmonic R&D, PMP lineage, software and research protocols, embodied facilitation, product and operations, and sound engineering. Julián De La Reta brings over 15 years of PMP practice, and a professional PMP training course has already been sold and validated as a standalone offering. Beacon exists as a working artifact and protocol, with nearly a year of free exploratory implementations across diverse settings. The team has completed market research and is now in Costa Rica. The missing layer is a closed paid Beacon or Beacon + PMP pilot. The current task is to convert accumulated practice and qualified interest into the first paid pilots.

## Slide 6 — Costa Rica Is An Accelerator For First Paid Pilots

**On slide**

Costa Rica combines premium wellness tourism, retreat infrastructure, Blue Zone/Nicoya positioning, and international demand.

Context: ~2.92M tourists in 2024, ~USD 5.4B tourism income, strong U.S. demand, ~9-10% wellness-market growth.

**Speaker note**

Costa Rica is a strong entry geography because the buyer context already exists. Boutique operators and retreat hosts need differentiation, international guests already pay for premium wellness formats, and the country has a wellness narrative that makes the category legible. These figures are context, not proof of product-market fit. Product-market fit still requires paid pilots, repeatability, and partner demand.

## Slide 7 — The Commercial Wedge Is Operator-Led

**On slide**

Primary wedge: Founder Pop-up / Signature Journey B2B2C.

USD 95 per person · USD 850 minimum · 8-15 participants · 75-90 minutes · 25-35% partner revenue share.

**Speaker note**

The first customer is not the tourist directly. It is the operator, retreat host, or hospitality partner who already has audience, space, trust, and calendar. The first sellable offer is a Founder Pop-up / Signature Journey B2B2C. Two secondary offers support that wedge: a 60-day Boutique Pilot at USD 5,000 founder pricing, and a Founding Beacon Facilitator path at USD 1,500. These are indicative founder ranges designed to test willingness to pay, operational fit, and repeatability.

## Slide 8 — The Main Risk Is Category Confusion

**On slide**

Risk: Beacon + PMP can be misread as therapy, sound bath, medical technology, ceremony, AI companion, or research validation.

Mitigation: disciplined non-clinical wellness lane, protocol, separated layers, facilitation levels.

**Speaker note**

The biggest risk is category confusion. If the market mixes the layers, the project creates legal, reputational, and commercial risk. The mitigation is the strategy: keep the lane deliberately non-clinical wellness, use a care-oriented protocol, separate Beacon, Beacon + PMP, app, research, data, and AI, and define facilitation levels before scaling. The app is already advanced, though not yet commercially launched. AI is a future operator-mediated support layer, not a current product.

## Slide 9 — The Ask Is Operational Access

**On slide**

Current ask: qualified introductions to boutique wellness operators, retreat hosts, and hospitality partners in Costa Rica.

Secondary ask: a local champion who can open, qualify, and filter those conversations.

**Speaker note**

Capital is a next-stage ask, after first paid pilots are closed and the format is validated. What matters now is operational access: qualified introductions to operators who can place first paid pilots, and a local champion who can help filter the right conversations. The near-term question is simple: can Beacon + PMP convert a serious body of practice into paid, repeatable, premium wellness formats in Costa Rica?
