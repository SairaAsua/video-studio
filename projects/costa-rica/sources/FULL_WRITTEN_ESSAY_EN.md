# Harmonic Beacon + Personal Myth Projection (PMP): Full Written Essay

> Written discussion essay for advisors, intermediaries, and potential strategic backers. It is not a final business plan, a fundraising document, financial or legal advice, or a clinical claim. It is a narrative and strategic framing of the current opportunity, the current stage, and the next operational ask.

## Deep Inner Journeys Became Premium Demand

In just a few years, deep inner journeys moved from cultural niche to international premium demand. The mental-health conversation went mainstream. The psychedelic renaissance culturally legitimized inner work. Wellness tourism learned to package transformation, not only rest. Guests who once asked for spa relaxation now ask for depth, meaning, self-knowledge, and guided experience.

Supply did not keep up. The deepest formats remain tied to substances or clinical settings. What hospitality can integrate safely and commercially often stays generic: sound baths, meditation rooms, background music, breathwork classes, or loosely framed rituals. Many of these formats are useful, but they are already widely available. For a premium operator, the problem is no longer whether guests understand wellness. The problem is whether the operator can offer something structured, differentiated, careful, and commercially legible without crossing into medical, therapeutic, or psychedelic-adjacent territory.

That is the structural gap Harmonic Beacon + Personal Myth Projection (PMP) is designed to address: guided, substance-free experiences that are deep enough to matter, clear enough to sell, and careful enough to become a category of their own.

## Beacon + PMP Is A Signature Wellness Experience

Beacon + PMP is a premium, substance-free, sound-symbolic wellness experience supported by a proprietary harmonic sound field and a structured symbolic process.

Beacon is a device and protocol for creating a proprietary harmonic field, implemented through sound and used under conditions of situated listening. It is not ambient background music, a generic sound bath, a recording device, or a medical treatment. A Beacon session is a non-clinical wellness experience in which participants listen within the Beacon harmonic field under a defined frame: preparation, attention, possibility of pause or exit, and minimal integration.

PMP is a symbolic and psychotherapeutic practice developed by Julián De La Reta, with over 15 years of commercial and professional practice and an already sold professional training course. That lineage matters. It gives the joint format depth, discipline, and symbolic structure. It also requires a precise boundary: in Costa Rica external materials, Beacon + PMP is framed as a wellness experience of exploration, not therapy.

The combination is the point. Beacon gives the experience a distinct harmonic environment. PMP gives it a guided symbolic process. Together, they create a format that is not merely sound, not merely conversation, not an app, and not a clinical service. It is a live, guided, substance-free inner journey that a premium wellness operator can host.

## The Discovery Came From Use

Beacon began as an experimental probe for Harmonic Information Theory: a way to make harmonic structure perceptible in embodied listening. It was not designed as a wellness product. Its first purpose was research.

The shift toward wellness came from use. Across nearly a year of free exploratory implementations, the team observed that people did not relate to Beacon as a neutral technical demo. They related to it as an experience. Beacon has been used in tai chi sessions held within the Beacon harmonic field, group experiences for dozens of participants at Edge City 2025, sessions in elder day-care centers, and weekly remote group sessions running for the past two months.

Edge City 2025 made the signal especially visible. People returned. They queued to experience the device again. They reported a range of positive subjective effects. Those reports are experiential accounts, not clinical evidence. Repeated voluntary interest is not traction, and free enthusiasm is not willingness to pay. But it is a meaningful discovery path. It showed qualitative pull, and it justified a disciplined commercial question: can this accumulated practice become a paid, repeatable, non-clinical wellness format?

That question now defines the current stage.

## The Current Stage Is Conservative By Design

The project begins from accumulated practice. It has artifacts, protocol, over 15 years of commercially practiced PMP, a sold professional PMP training course, nearly a year of free exploratory Beacon implementations, market research, a clear commercial sequence, and a team in Costa Rica now placing the first paid Beacon and Beacon + PMP pilots.

The project also has a hard boundary: it does not yet have closed paid Beacon or Beacon + PMP pilots, and it does not yet have commercial validation for the combined package.

This distinction is central. PMP standalone practice and PMP professional training are commercially validated layers. Beacon and the combined Beacon + PMP package are not commercially validated yet. The current stage is pre-first-paid-pilot: the team is in Costa Rica, placing the first paid pilots now.

That is not a weakness to hide. It is the correct order of claims. The project has enough accumulated practice to justify the test, and not enough paid validation to pretend the test is complete.

## Costa Rica Is An Accelerator, Not Validation

Costa Rica is a strong entry geography because it combines premium wellness tourism, international guests, retreat infrastructure, Blue Zone/Nicoya positioning, and a country-level wellness narrative.

The market context is unusually aligned. Costa Rica received about 2.92 million tourists in 2024. Tourism income was about USD 5.4 billion. A large share of arrivals and tourism income comes from the United States. The country has an official "Wellness Pura Vida" positioning signal, and Nicoya carries Blue Zone brand value. Wellness-market growth has been reported in the 9-10% annual range in recent years.

These figures are useful context, not proof of demand for Beacon. No Costa Rica figure proves product-market fit. Product-market fit requires paid pilots, repeatability, and partner demand. But the context matters: Costa Rica has the kind of operators, guests, language, retreat infrastructure, and premium wellness expectations that can accelerate first paid pilots.

The initial buyer is therefore not the tourist directly. The first buyer is the boutique wellness operator, retreat host, or hospitality partner who already has audience, space, trust, calendar, and a reason to differentiate. The commercial path reaches the premium guest through operators and retreat hosts first, before any direct-to-consumer acquisition.

## The Commercial Wedge Is Paid And Operator-Led

The primary wedge is the Founder Pop-up / Signature Journey B2B2C: a 75-90 minute experience for 8-15 participants, priced at USD 95 per person with a USD 850 minimum. If the partner brings the audience, the partner revenue share is 25-35%.

This offer is small enough to place quickly and concrete enough to test actual willingness to pay. It does not ask an operator to adopt a full platform, license a complex system, or believe a large forecast. It asks a simpler question: would this belong in your programming, at this price, for this guest, under this operational frame?

Two secondary offers support the same wedge. The first is a 60-day Boutique Pilot at USD 5,000 founder pricing: four group sessions, one staff demo, co-branded materials, basic operational training, an acceptance report, and a continuity proposal. The second is a Founding Beacon Facilitator path at USD 1,500, with initial training, a six-month license, materials, community, and limited commercial use for non-clinical Beacon wellness formats.

These are founder pricing ranges, not final tariff sheets. Their purpose is to test willingness to pay, operational fit, and repeatability. The immediate commercial discipline is simple: every qualified conversation should end in a proposal, date, price, or no. Good meetings do not count as traction.

## The Sequence Protects The Project

The project has a broader roadmap, but the roadmap is intentionally sequenced.

The app is already advanced, though not yet commercially launched. It should be framed as continuity after the live experience, not as the first acquisition front. PMP-GPT and AI are future, operator-mediated support layers for continuity and personalization. AI is not a current product, not an AI therapist, and not a replacement for human facilitation.

Facilitator scaling, site kits, international licensing, and marketplace logic come later. They require paid pilots, protocol refinement, selection criteria, quality control, and evidence of repeatable partner demand. The project should not skip those steps, because skipping them would create exactly the category confusion and overclaiming risk the strategy is designed to avoid.

## The Main Risk Is Category Confusion

Beacon + PMP can be misunderstood as therapy, sound bath, medical technology, music, psychedelic-adjacent ceremony, AI companion, or research validation.

That risk is not incidental. It is the core risk to manage. If the market mixes the layers, the project creates legal, reputational, and commercial exposure. A partner may sell it as therapy. A funder may read it as a clinical claim. A guest may expect a medical outcome. A facilitator may overextend the format. An AI layer may be pulled too early into a mental-health frame.

The mitigation is the strategy: keep the lane deliberately non-clinical wellness; use a care-oriented protocol; keep language disciplined; separate Beacon, Beacon + PMP, app, research, data, and AI; and define facilitation levels before scaling.

This discipline is not a limitation of the project. It is the condition that makes the project legible to operators, funders, and responsible partners.

## The Current Ask Is Operational Access

The current ask is not capital. Capital is a next-stage ask, explicitly conditional on first paid pilots being closed and the format being validated.

The current ask is operational: qualified introductions to boutique wellness operators, retreat hosts, and hospitality partners in Costa Rica who can help place the first paid pilots. The secondary ask is a local champion who can open, qualify, and filter those conversations before the team spends time on them.

That ask matches the current stage. The project does not need a story that says everything is already proven. It needs the right operators, the right rooms, the right first paid pilots, and disciplined feedback on price, fit, repeatability, and positioning.

The near-term question is simple: can Beacon + PMP convert a serious body of practice into paid, repeatable, premium wellness formats in Costa Rica?
