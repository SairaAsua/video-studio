# Harmonic Beacon + Personal Myth Projection (PMP): 2-4 Page Pitch Deck

> Compact deck content for advisors, intermediaries, and potential strategic backers. Not a final business plan, not financial or legal advice, and not a clinical claim.

## Page 1 — Why Now / The Gap

### Slide 1 — Deep Inner Journeys Entered Premium Demand

**On slide**

Deep inner journeys moved from niche to premium demand. Hospitality still lacks a careful, substance-free format it can integrate.

**Talk track**

In just a few years, deep inner journeys moved from cultural niche to international premium demand: mental health went mainstream, the psychedelic renaissance culturally legitimized inner work, and wellness tourism learned to sell it. The deepest formats still tend to sit in substances or clinical settings, while hospitality often gets generic sound or relaxation formats.

### Slide 2 — The Competition Validated The Market And Left A Gap

**On slide**

Sound baths became a commodity. Neuroacoustic and wellness apps validated demand. The gap is commercially legible depth.

**Talk track**

The competition matters because it validated the market. Sound baths showed that guests understand sound-led wellness, but they also became hard to differentiate. Neuroacoustic and wellness apps showed demand for structured state-change experiences, but they are not a live signature format an operator can own. Harmonic Beacon + Personal Myth Projection (PMP) is built for the gap they leave: a premium, substance-free, sound-symbolic wellness experience supported by a proprietary harmonic sound field and a structured symbolic process.

### Slide 3 — Beacon + PMP Is A Signature Wellness Experience

**On slide**

Beacon creates a proprietary harmonic field. PMP gives the experience symbolic structure. The format is wellness, not therapy.

**Talk track**

Beacon is a device and protocol for creating a proprietary harmonic field through sound under situated listening. PMP is a symbolic and psychotherapeutic practice developed by Julián De La Reta, with over 15 years of commercial and professional practice and an already sold professional training course. In Costa Rica, the combined format is framed as non-clinical wellness: exploration, care, and integration, without medical or therapeutic claims.

## Page 2 — Discovery / Operational Base / Market

### Slide 4 — The Discovery Came From Use

**On slide**

Beacon began as a HIT experimental probe. Edge City showed qualitative pull: people returned, queued, and reported positive subjective effects.

**Talk track**

Beacon began as an experimental probe for Harmonic Information Theory: a way to make harmonic structure perceptible in embodied listening. Its first purpose was research, not wellness commerce. During exploratory sessions, especially at Edge City 2025, an unexpected pattern emerged: people returned, queued to experience the device again, and reported positive subjective effects. Those reports are experiential accounts, not clinical evidence. They explain why the team began testing Beacon as a disciplined wellness format.

### Slide 5 — The Operational Base Is Real; Paid Pilots Are Next

**On slide**

Exists today: PMP practice, sold PMP training, Beacon artifact/protocol, free exploratory implementations, market research, team in Costa Rica.

Current stage: pre-first-paid-pilot, placing the first paid pilots now.

**Talk track**

The base is real, and the commercial claim stays conservative. The project has artifacts, protocol, over 15 years of commercially practiced PMP, a sold professional PMP training course, nearly a year of free exploratory Beacon implementations, market research, a clear commercial sequence, and a team in Costa Rica now placing the first paid Beacon and Beacon + PMP pilots. It does not yet have closed paid Beacon or Beacon + PMP pilots, or commercial validation for the combined package.

### Slide 6 — Costa Rica Is An Accelerator, Not Validation

**On slide**

Costa Rica combines premium wellness tourism, retreat infrastructure, Blue Zone/Nicoya positioning, and international demand.

Context: ~2.92M tourists in 2024 · ~USD 5.4B tourism income · strong U.S. demand · ~9-10% wellness-market growth.

**Talk track**

Costa Rica is a strong entry geography because the buyer context already exists. Boutique operators and retreat hosts need differentiation, international guests already pay for premium wellness formats, and the country has a wellness narrative that makes the category legible. These figures are context, not proof of product-market fit. Product-market fit requires paid pilots, repeatability, and partner demand.

## Page 3 — Buyer / Offer / Risk

### Slide 7 — The First Buyer Already Has Audience And Trust

**On slide**

Primary buyer: boutique wellness operators in Costa Rica, especially Nicoya, Nosara, Guanacaste, and La Fortuna.

Adjacent buyer: retreat hosts who bring groups and shape programming.

**Talk track**

The first customer is whoever already has audience, space, trust, calendar, and a reason to differentiate. The wellness tourist is the end user, not the first direct customer. The commercial path reaches the premium guest through operators and retreat hosts first, before any direct-to-consumer acquisition.

### Slide 8 — The Commercial Wedge Is A Paid Operator-Led Pilot

**On slide**

Primary offer: Founder Pop-up / Signature Journey B2B2C.

USD 95 per person · USD 850 minimum · 8-15 participants · 75-90 minutes · 25-35% partner revenue share.

**Talk track**

The first sellable offer is a Founder Pop-up / Signature Journey B2B2C. Two secondary offers support that wedge without turning the project into an over-menu: a 60-day Boutique Pilot at USD 5,000 founder pricing, and a Founding Beacon Facilitator path at USD 1,500. These are indicative founder ranges designed to test willingness to pay, operational fit, and repeatability.

### Slide 9 — The Main Risk Is Category Confusion

**On slide**

Risk: Beacon + PMP can be misread as therapy, sound bath, medical technology, ceremony, AI companion, or research validation.

Mitigation: disciplined non-clinical wellness lane, protocol, separated layers, facilitation levels.

**Talk track**

The biggest risk is category confusion. If the market mixes the layers, the project creates legal, reputational, and commercial risk. The mitigation is the strategy: keep the lane deliberately non-clinical wellness, use a care-oriented protocol, separate Beacon, Beacon + PMP, app, research, data, and AI, and define facilitation levels before scaling.

### Slide 11 — The Sequence Protects The Project From Overclaiming

**On slide**

Now: place first paid pilots.

Next: repeatability, protocol refinement, operator fit.

Later: app continuity, facilitator network, site kits, licensing, AI as future operator-mediated support.

**Talk track**

The sequence is deliberate. The app is already advanced, though not yet commercially launched, and should be continuity after the live experience. AI is a future operator-mediated support layer, not a current product and not a replacement for human facilitation. Facilitator scaling, site kits, licensing, and marketplace logic come after paid pilots and protocol refinement.

### Slide 12 — The Ask Is Operational Access

**On slide**

Current ask: qualified introductions to boutique wellness operators, retreat hosts, and hospitality partners in Costa Rica.

Secondary ask: a local champion who can open, qualify, and filter those conversations.

**Talk track**

Capital is a next-stage ask, after first paid pilots are closed and the format is validated. What matters now is operational access: qualified introductions to operators who can place first paid pilots, plus a local champion who can filter the right conversations. The near-term question is simple: can Beacon + PMP convert a serious body of practice into paid, repeatable, premium wellness formats in Costa Rica?
