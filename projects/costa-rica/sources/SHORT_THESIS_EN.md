# Harmonic Beacon + Personal Myth Projection (PMP): Short Thesis

> A concise discussion thesis for advisors, intermediaries, and potential strategic backers. It is not a final business plan, a fundraising document, financial or legal advice, or a clinical claim.

In just a few years, deep inner journeys moved from cultural niche to international premium demand: the mental-health conversation went mainstream, the psychedelic renaissance culturally legitimized inner work, and wellness tourism learned to sell it. Supply did not keep up. The deepest formats remain tied to substances or clinical settings, while what hospitality can actually integrate often stays generic.

That leaves a structural gap: guided, substance-free experiences that are commercially legible for premium wellness operators and careful enough to become a category of their own.

Harmonic Beacon + Personal Myth Projection (PMP) is built for that gap: a premium, substance-free, sound-symbolic wellness experience supported by a proprietary harmonic sound field and a structured symbolic process.

The immediate market runs through boutique wellness operators, retreat hosts, and hospitality partners in Costa Rica who already have the audience, the space, the trust, and the need to differentiate. The first task is to place paid pilots through those operators before selling an app, launching an AI product, or building a mass consumer funnel.

## The Project Begins From Accumulated Practice

PMP has over 15 years of commercial and professional practice behind it, and a professional PMP training course has already been sold and validated as a standalone offering. Beacon exists as a working device and protocol, with nearly a year of free exploratory implementations across diverse settings. The core Beacon team within AlterMundi combines HIT authorship, harmonic R&D, PMP lineage, software and research protocols, embodied facilitation, product and operations, and sound engineering. The team has completed market research, defined a commercial sequence, and is currently in Costa Rica.

Beacon also has a clear discovery path. It began as an experimental probe for Harmonic Information Theory: a way to make harmonic structure perceptible in embodied listening. During exploratory sessions, especially at Edge City 2025, an unexpected pattern emerged: people returned, queued to experience the device again, and reported positive subjective effects. Those reports are experiential accounts, not clinical evidence. They explain why the team began exploring Beacon as a disciplined, non-clinical wellness format.

The current stage is still deliberately conservative: the team is in Costa Rica, pre-first-paid-pilot, placing the first paid pilots now. Beacon and the combined Beacon + PMP format do not yet have closed paid pilots or commercial validation as a packaged offer.

That distinction matters. PMP brings lineage, professional practice, and seriousness. The Costa Rica Beacon + PMP formats are framed as wellness, not therapy. The commercial question now is whether this accumulated practice can become a repeatable paid format for premium operators.

## The Commercial Wedge Is A Paid Operator-Led Pilot

The first sellable offer is the Founder Pop-up / Signature Journey B2B2C: a 75-90 minute experience for 8-15 participants, priced at USD 95 per person with a USD 850 minimum, with a 25-35% revenue share when the partner brings the audience.

Two secondary offers support the same wedge without diluting it:

- a 60-day Boutique Pilot at USD 5,000 founder pricing;
- a Founding Beacon Facilitator path at USD 1,500, with a later USD 149/month license layer and 10-15% participation.

These are founder pricing ranges, not final tariff sheets. Their purpose is to test willingness to pay, operational fit, and repeatability.

The roadmap is intentionally sequenced: app continuity after the live experience (the app is already advanced, though not yet commercially launched), AI as a future operator-mediated support layer, facilitator scaling, site kits, licensing, and marketplace logic only after the paid pilot layer is real.

## Costa Rica Is An Accelerator, Not Validation

Costa Rica is a strong entry geography because it combines premium wellness tourism, international guests, retreat infrastructure, Blue Zone/Nicoya positioning, and a country-level wellness narrative. Public and market research context points to about 2.92 million tourists in 2024, about USD 5.4 billion in tourism income, strong U.S. demand, and wellness-market growth in the 9-10% annual range in recent years.

Those figures do not prove product-market fit. They explain why Costa Rica is an unusually good place to test first paid pilots: the category context is already educated, the buyer exists, and the guest is already paying for premium wellness formats.

## The Main Risk Is Category Confusion

Beacon + PMP can be misread as therapy, a generic sound bath, medical technology, music, psychedelic-adjacent ceremony, AI companion, or research validation.

The mitigation is to keep the lane deliberately non-clinical wellness; use a care-oriented protocol; keep language disciplined; separate Beacon, Beacon + PMP, app, research, data, and AI; and define facilitation levels before scaling.

That discipline is not a limitation of the project. It is the condition that makes the project legible to operators, funders, and responsible partners.

## The Current Ask Is Operational Access

Capital belongs to the next stage, after first paid pilots are closed and the format is validated.

The current ask is operational access: qualified introductions to boutique wellness operators, retreat hosts, and hospitality partners in Costa Rica who can help place the first paid pilots, plus a local champion who can open, qualify, and filter those conversations.

The near-term question is simple: can Beacon + PMP convert a serious body of practice into paid, repeatable, premium wellness formats in Costa Rica?
