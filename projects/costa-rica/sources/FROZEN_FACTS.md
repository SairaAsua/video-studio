## 10. Risks And Mitigations

Primary risk:

> Category confusion / claims discipline.

Why it matters:

Beacon + PMP can be misunderstood as therapy, sound bath, medical technology, music, psychedelic-adjacent ceremony, AI companion, or research validation. If the category is not controlled, the market will mix the layers and create legal, reputational, and commercial risk.

Primary mitigation:

> Keep the lane deliberately non-clinical wellness; use a care-oriented protocol; keep language disciplined; separate Beacon, Beacon + PMP, app, research, data, and AI; define facilitation levels before scaling.

Secondary risks:

| Risk | Preferred use | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Enthusiasm without payment | Go-to-market and B2B operator pieces | Every qualified conversation ends in a proposal, date, price, or no. Good meetings do not count as traction. |
| Presential format scales poorly by design | Investor, scalability, and operations pieces | Scale through selected facilitators, licensing, site kits, and continuity layers only after paid pilots and protocol refinement. |
| Underpriced positioning | Pricing and operator pieces | Use founder pricing with explicit expiration; keep the first offer premium enough to avoid sound-bath commoditization. |
| App or AI pushed too early | Investor and strategy pieces | Keep app as continuity after live experience; keep AI as future operator-mediated layer. |
