# SPINE — Beacon+PMP in Costa Rica

Each beat = ~50–80 seconds. Voiceover narration over visuals.

---

## Beat 1 — THE CHANGE (0:00–1:00)

> The deep inner journey moved from niche to premium demand. The supply didn't follow.

**What we say:**
Wellness travel is no longer a spa add-on. It's a $1.2 billion market growing at double digits — and the most sought-after experiences are substance-free, structured, and premium. Retreat centers are full. Boutique operators are scrambling to differentiate. But what's missing is a signature inner journey: proprietary sound technology, symbolic structure, and a careful protocol — no substances, no pseudoscience, no generic sound bath.

That's the gap. That's why Costa Rica. That's why now.

**Suggested visuals:**
- Opening: a dark screen, a single warm light emerges — the Beacon
- Fast sequence: retreat spaces worldwide → luxury wellness → people in stillness
- Costa Rica establishing shot: Pacific coast at golden hour, Nosara, tropical forest
- [FAL: premium wellness space, Costa Rica, sunset, bamboo architecture, warm light, photorealistic]

**What we do NOT say:**
- No "we discovered the Blue Zone"
- No pseudoscientific claims
- No disparaging comparisons with competitors

## Beat 2 — THE GAP (1:00–1:50)

> The market is full of sound baths, apps, and generic retreats. The premium, sober, substance-free space is empty.

**What we say:**
Costa Rica already has everything: yoga, breathwork, sound bowls, Reiki, plant medicine, cold plunge, ecstatic dance. But boutique operators — hotels, retreat centers, retreat hosts — need differentiation. What doesn't exist is a premium, substance-free inner journey experience with proprietary sound technology, symbolic structure, and a careful protocol. That's the gap.

**Suggested visuals:**
- Mosaic of generic wellness offerings (bowls, apps, massages)
- The Beacon powering on at center — first close-up of the device
- [FAL: sound bath generic vs Beacon premium contrast — same space, two levels of intention, split frame]

**What we do NOT say:**

- No "we are better"
- No competitor names

## Beat 3 — THE OFFER (1:50–3:10)

> Three formats. Simple. Sellable today. Nothing to build that isn't ready.

**What we say:**
We don't need 15 product lines. We need three sellable offers:

1. **Pop-up Beacon+PMP** — A 75–90 minute signature experience inside existing retreats. The partner provides space and audience; we provide Beacon, facilitation, protocol, and materials. Public price: USD 85–150 per person. Minimum for us: USD 750–1,200 per session.

2. **Anchor B2B pilot** — 60 days with an eco-resort or retreat center. We turn Beacon into a pilot amenity: measure acceptance, willingness to pay, qualitative reviews. Founder price: USD 5,000.

3. **Founding Facilitator cohort** — A small, curated first group of ambassador facilitators with license, support, and limited commercial use rights. Founder price: USD 1,500.

**Suggested visuals:**
- Three visual cards (premium style, clean typography) — one per offer
- Pop-up: small group in a palapa with the Beacon playing
- Pilot: boutique hotel lobby, calendar, handshake
- Facilitator: person guiding with presence, Beacon glowing

**What we do NOT say:**
- No hardware pricing or app pricing
- No PMP-GPT or AI yet
- No "mass certification"

## Beat 4 — THE RIGOR (3:10–4:10)

> This is not improvisation. There are 44 pages of operational analysis, 69 directives, buyer personas, protocols, and governance behind every decision.

**What we say:**
We did the work. We analyzed the Costa Rican wellness market in depth. We interviewed the ecosystem. We modeled buyer personas, pricing, channels, risks. We designed protocols for care, consent, pause criteria, and referral pathways. We separated Beacon wellness from PMP psychotherapeutic work with legal clarity. We documented what language to use and what promises not to make. All of this is written and reviewed.

The result is simple but not simplistic: every price, every format, every boundary has a foundation.

- Documents stacked → unfold → converge into a clear map
- Governance icons: data, consent, protocol, care
- Key phrase on screen: "We don't promise cures. We design listening experiences with a proprietary harmonic acoustic field and responsible documentation."
- [FAL: elegant workspace with documents, laptop, Beacon powered off — the analytical layer behind the experience]

**What we do NOT say:**
- No listing the 69 directives
- No diving into HIT, Jung, Moreno, Campbell, EEG/HRV
- No "we're more rigorous than anyone"
- No competitor names

## Beat 5 — THE DISCIPLINE (4:10–4:55)

> What we choose not to do is as important as what we do.

**What we say:**
We won't sell standalone hardware. We won't mass-certify facilitators. We won't put the app or AI up front before validating the in-person experience. We won't make clinical or pseudoscientific claims. We won't give away the experience to be liked. We won't mix Beacon with substances. We won't treat Costa Rica as extractive decor.

Each "no" protects the value of the "yes."

**Suggested visuals:**
- Sequence of "NO" → transform into a shield or seal
- Contrast: what the market does (noise, claims, saturation) vs. what Beacon chooses (quiet, precision, depth)
- [FAL: minimalist shield design, premium gold-on-black aesthetic]

**What we do NOT say:**
- No defensive tone
- No "the market is full of charlatans"
- No more than 5 "no"s on screen at once

---

## Beat 6 — THE CLOSE (4:55–5:45)

> The in-person Beacon sells trust. Training multiplies distribution. Licensing captures recurrence. The app retains. AI scales later. But the first step is in-person, paid, and carefully held. That's what we're doing in Costa Rica right now.

**What we say:**
We know the right order. We're not going to invert it. First: premium paid experiences with boutique operators. Then: facilitator network. Then: app as continuity. Then: AI as horizon.

We're looking for investors who understand this isn't an app-disruption play or a mass-hardware play — it's a layered rollout where each stage funds the next, and where the core asset isn't the technology but the trust built through in-person experience.

**A note on risks:**
The principal risk in this phase is not the market — Costa Rica is ready. It's execution discipline: resisting the temptation to open too many fronts before the first paid pilots close. Our mitigation is the sequenced roadmap you just heard. No app before paid validation. No mass certification before protocol is proven. No AI layer before human facilitation is stable.

If you're interested in being part of the founding partner circle, let's talk.

**Suggested visuals:**
- Ascending sequence: Beacon → people → network → devices → light
- Final frame: Beacon lit, contact information
- [FAL: the Beacon glowing in a dark premium space, warm amber light, invitation feeling]

**What we do NOT say:**
- No "invest now"
- No "this is going to be huge"
- No unvalidated financial projections
- No artificial urgency
