## Frame

**Audience:** US investors, advisors, intermediaries.
**Tone:** positive, executive, contemplative. Sober, premium, non-clinical.
**Pacing:** ~50 seconds per beat, 5 minutes total. Each beat has its own
breath. No shot rushes; the camera always lingers.
**Opening:** the change in the world, not the product. (estilo-wellness rule 6.)
**Closing:** explicit ask + a self-proposed risk with mitigation. (rule 8.)

**Naming convention (FROZEN_FACTS):** First mention of the full program
is **Harmonic Beacon + Personal Myth Projection (PMP)**. After that,
**Beacon + PMP**. The device alone, after first mention, is **Beacon**.

**Status discipline (FROZEN_FACTS):** we are placing the first paid pilots
in Costa Rica. Pre-first-paid-pilot. Beacon is not commercially validated
yet. PMP has 15+ years of commercial practice. The app is advanced but
not launched. AI is a future operator-mediated layer.

## Beat 1 — THE CHANGE (0:00–0:50)

**The world moved first.** Mental health went mainstream, the psychedelic
renaissance culturally legitimized inner work, and wellness tourism
learned to sell it. The supply side did not keep up.

> In just a few years, deep inner journeys moved from cultural niche to
> international premium demand. Mental health went mainstream, the
> psychedelic renaissance legitimized inner work culturally, and wellness
> tourism learned to sell it. The supply side did not keep up. The
> deepest formats remain tied to substances or clinical settings, while
> what hospitality can actually integrate often stays generic.

over the canopy. The world opening, the breath before.

## Beat 2 — THE GAP (0:50–1:40)

**The gap is commercial.** Sound baths became a commodity. Apps validated
demand but aren't a live signature format an operator can own. The
structural gap is *commercially legible depth* — guided, substance-free
experiences careful enough to become a category of their own.

> That leaves a structural gap: guided, substance-free experiences
> commercially legible for premium wellness operators and careful enough
> to become a category of their own. Harmonic Beacon + Personal Myth
> Projection (PMP) is built for that gap: a premium, substance-free,
> sound-symbolic wellness experience supported by a proprietary harmonic
> sound field and a structured symbolic process.

**Visual:** Interior listening room at dusk — the Beacon as a sculptural
anchor in a dim wooden space, single warm light source, no people
visible. Negative space for text.

**Motion preset:** `zoom_in` (slow push toward the device, attention gathers).

offers — a 60-day boutique pilot at USD 5,000 and a Founding Beacon
Facilitator path at USD 1,500 — support the wedge without diluting it.

> Three founder offers, one wedge. The primary: a Founder Pop-up

## Beat 4 — THE RIGOR (2:50–3:50)

**This didn't start as a product.** Beacon began as an experimental
probe for Harmonic Information Theory — a way to make harmonic structure
perceptible in embodied listening. During exploratory sessions, most
visibly at Edge City 2025, an unexpected pattern emerged: people returned,
queued to experience the device again, reported positive subjective
effects. Those reports are experiential accounts, not clinical evidence.
That is the discovery path that led the team to test Beacon as a
disciplined non-clinical wellness format.

> Beacon began as a probe for Harmonic Information Theory — a way to
> make harmonic structure perceptible in embodied listening. During
> exploratory sessions, especially at Edge City 2025, an unexpected
> pattern emerged: people returned, queued to experience the device
> again, and reported positive subjective effects. Those reports are
> experiential accounts, not clinical evidence. They explain why we
> began testing Beacon as a disciplined, non-clinical wellness format.

**Visual:** A detail shot — wood grain, a hand resting, water in a stone
bowl, the device seen close. The materials of the practice.

**Motion preset:** `push_in_right` (leaning in from a different angle).

## Beat 5 — THE DISCIPLINE (3:50–4:40)

**Each "no" protects the value of the "yes."** No standalone hardware
sales. No mass facilitator certification. No clinical or pseudoscientific
claims. No AI therapist. No mixing with substances. No treating Costa
Rica as extractive decor. The app is advanced, not launched. AI is a
future operator-mediated layer. The lane is deliberately non-clinical
wellness; the discipline is the condition that makes the project legible
to operators, funders, and partners.

> Each no protects the value of the yes. We won't sell standalone
> hardware. We won't mass-certify facilitators. We won't put the app
> or AI up front before validating the in-person experience. We won't
> make clinical or pseudoscientific claims. We won't mix Beacon with
> substances. The app is advanced, not launched. AI is a future
> operator-mediated layer. The lane is deliberately non-clinical
> wellness — that discipline is the condition that makes the project
> legible.

**Visual:** A wider contemplative shot — open architecture (palapa,
pavilion, doorway) with a single figure in silhouette or absent. The
restraint, the negative space, the room that exists for the practice.

## Beat 6 — THE CLOSE (4:40–5:30)

**Costa Rica is an accelerator, not validation.** ~2.92M tourists in
2024, ~USD 5.4B tourism income, strong US demand, wellness-market growth
9-10% annually. The buyer context exists; the country narrative is
aligned. Product-market fit still requires paid pilots, repeatability,
and partner demand.

**The risk is execution discipline.** The principal risk in this phase
is not the market — Costa Rica is ready. It is execution discipline:
resisting the temptation to open too many fronts before the first paid
pilots close. Mitigation is the sequenced roadmap you just heard.

**The ask is operational access.** Qualified introductions to boutique
wellness operators, retreat hosts, and hospitality partners in Costa
Rica who can help place the first paid pilots. A local champion who
can open, qualify, and filter those conversations.

> Costa Rica is an accelerator, not validation. The buyer context

> the first paid pilots close. The ask is operational access:

  space / ambient sound before the first kinetic text appears.

  mid-beat (around 30% through), staying for the rest of the beat.
  Fade-in 0.6s, slide-up 30px, no fade-out (cuts are premium).

  give the listener time to absorb.
