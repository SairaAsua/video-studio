## Scene 1 — Beat 1 — THE CHANGE (0:00–0:50)

In just a few years, deep inner journeys moved from cultural niche to
international premium demand. Mental health went mainstream, the
psychedelic renaissance legitimized inner work culturally, and wellness
tourism learned to sell it. The supply side did not keep up. The
deepest formats remain tied to substances or clinical settings, while
what hospitality can actually integrate often stays generic.

---

## Scene 2 — Beat 2 — THE GAP (0:50–1:40)

That leaves a structural gap: guided, substance-free experiences
commercially legible for premium wellness operators and careful enough
to become a category of their own. Harmonic Beacon + Personal Myth
Projection (PMP) is built for that gap: a premium, substance-free,
sound-symbolic wellness experience supported by a proprietary harmonic
sound field and a structured symbolic process.

---

## Scene 3 — Beat 3 — THE OFFER (1:40–2:50)

Three founder offers, one wedge. The primary: a Founder Pop-up
Signature Journey, 75 to 90 minutes for eight to fifteen participants,
USD 95 per person with an USD 850 minimum and a 25 to 35% partner
revenue share. A 60-day Boutique Pilot at USD 5,000 founder pricing
and a Founding Beacon Facilitator path at USD 1,500 hold the wedge
without turning the project into a menu. These are indicative founder
ranges, designed to test willingness to pay, operational fit, and
repeatability.

---

## Scene 4 — Beat 4 — THE RIGOR (2:50–3:50)

Beacon began as a probe for Harmonic Information Theory — a way to
make harmonic structure perceptible in embodied listening. During
exploratory sessions, especially at Edge City 2025, an unexpected
pattern emerged: people returned, queued to experience the device
again, and reported positive subjective effects. Those reports are
experiential accounts, not clinical evidence. They explain why we
began testing Beacon as a disciplined, non-clinical wellness format.

---

## Scene 5 — Beat 5 — THE DISCIPLINE (3:50–4:40)

Each no protects the value of the yes. We won't sell standalone
hardware. We won't mass-certify facilitators. We won't put the app
or AI up front before validating the in-person experience. We won't
make clinical or pseudoscientific claims. We won't mix Beacon with
substances. The app is advanced, not launched. AI is a future
operator-mediated layer. The lane is deliberately non-clinical
wellness — that discipline is the condition that makes the project
legible.

---

## Scene 6 — Beat 6 — THE CLOSE (4:40–5:30)

Costa Rica is an accelerator, not validation. The buyer context
exists; the country narrative is aligned. The principal risk in
this phase is not the market — Costa Rica is ready. It is execution
discipline: resisting the temptation to open too many fronts before
the first paid pilots close. The ask is operational access:
qualified introductions to boutique operators and retreat hosts in
Costa Rica who can place the first paid pilots, and a local champion
who can filter the right conversations. The near-term question is
simple: can Beacon + PMP convert a serious body of practice into
paid, repeatable, premium wellness formats in Costa Rica?
