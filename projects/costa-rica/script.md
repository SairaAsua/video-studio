# Beacon+PMP Costa Rica — Investor Pitch Script

## Scene 1 — Beat 1 — THE CHANGE (0:00–1:00)

Wellness travel is no longer a spa add-on. It's a $1.2 billion market growing at double digits — and the most sought-after experiences are substance-free, structured, and premium. Retreat centers are full. Boutique operators are scrambling to differentiate. But what's missing is a signature inner journey: proprietary sound technology, symbolic structure, and a careful protocol — no substances, no pseudoscience, no generic sound bath. That's the gap. That's why Costa Rica. That's why now.

## Scene 2 — Beat 2 — THE GAP (1:00–1:50)

Costa Rica already has everything: yoga, breathwork, sound bowls, Reiki, plant medicine, cold plunge, ecstatic dance. But boutique operators — hotels, retreat centers, retreat hosts — need differentiation. What doesn't exist is a premium, substance-free inner journey experience with proprietary sound technology, symbolic structure, and a careful protocol. That's the gap.

## Scene 3 — Beat 3 — THE OFFER (1:50–3:10)

We don't need 15 product lines. We need three sellable offers: 1. **Pop-up Beacon+PMP** — A 75–90 minute signature experience inside existing retreats. The partner provides space and audience; we provide Beacon, facilitation, protocol, and materials. Public price: USD 85–150 per person. Minimum for us: USD 750–1,200 per session. 2. **Anchor B2B pilot** — 60 days with an eco-resort or retreat center. We turn Beacon into a pilot amenity: measure acceptance, willingness to pay, qualitative reviews. Founder price: USD 5,000. 3. **Founding Facilitator cohort** — A small, curated first group of ambassador facilitators with license, support, and limited commercial use rights. Founder price: USD 1,500.

## Scene 4 — Beat 4 — THE RIGOR (3:10–4:10)

We did the work. We analyzed the Costa Rican wellness market in depth. We interviewed the ecosystem. We modeled buyer personas, pricing, channels, risks. We designed protocols for care, consent, pause criteria, and referral pathways. We separated Beacon wellness from PMP psychotherapeutic work with legal clarity. We documented what language to use and what promises not to make. All of this is written and reviewed. The result is simple but not simplistic: every price, every format, every boundary has a foundation.

## Scene 5 — Beat 5 — THE DISCIPLINE (4:10–4:55)

We won't sell standalone hardware. We won't mass-certify facilitators. We won't put the app or AI up front before validating the in-person experience. We won't make clinical or pseudoscientific claims. We won't give away the experience to be liked. We won't mix Beacon with substances. We won't treat Costa Rica as extractive decor. Each "no" protects the value of the "yes."

## Scene 6 — Beat 6 — THE CLOSE (4:55–5:45)

We know the right order. We're not going to invert it. First: premium paid experiences with boutique operators. Then: facilitator network. Then: app as continuity. Then: AI as horizon. We're looking for investors who understand this isn't an app-disruption play or a mass-hardware play — it's a layered rollout where each stage funds the next, and where the core asset isn't the technology but the trust built through in-person experience. **A note on risks:** The principal risk in this phase is not the market — Costa Rica is ready. It's execution discipline: resisting the temptation to open too many fronts before the first paid pilots close. Our mitigation is the sequenced roadmap you just heard. No app before paid validation. No mass certification before protocol is proven. No AI layer before human facilitation is stable. If you're interested in being part of the founding partner circle, let's talk.
