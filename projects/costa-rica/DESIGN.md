## What NOT to Do

- No AI-slides look (generic gradient backgrounds, floating glass panels, stock-photo people)
- No new-age aesthetic (purple chakras, mandalas, floating crystals, auroras)
- No text-heavy frames (this is cinema, not a deck)
- No saturated Instagram filters
- No drone shots that feel like real estate listings
- No human faces (silhouettes OK, backs of heads OK)
- No "tech startup" aesthetic (blue glow, circuit boards, server rooms)
