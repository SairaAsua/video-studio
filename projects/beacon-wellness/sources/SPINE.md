## Visual strategy per scene

Each scene opens with the **slide as a still anchor** (the user sees the deck visual for 3-4s as "thesis card"). Then we transition via zoom/pan into a Grok-generated **video b-roll fragment** that dramatizes the concept (4-6s). Return to a re-framed anchor at the end (caption-driven close). Captions throughout drive the narration.

Asset plan per scene (8 assets × 7 scenes = 56 assets, but anchor counts as both first and last block):
- 1 anchor still (re-purposed PDF slide page, or re-generated stylistically)
- 2-3 video fragments (Grok video, 4-6s each, 720p)
- 1 caption track
- Total scene duration = 20s

## Style canon

- Dark, gold/bronze palette, premium texture
- No clinical/medical cues
- "Live, signature wellness journey" framing
- "Substance-free, structured, sound-symbolic"
- No hype: no "revolutionary", "transformative", "disruptive"
- Costa Rica = accelerator, not validation
- The ask is **operational access**, not capital
